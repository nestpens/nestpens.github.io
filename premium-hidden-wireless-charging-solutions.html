Preventing Mechanical Wear

Over time, this mechanical stress bends the internal pins and degrades the connection. Dirt and lint also get compacted into the socket. You eventually face costly repair bills or forced early upgrades just to keep your phone functioning.

Wireless charging bypasses this mechanical wear completely. Power transfers safely through magnetic induction. You never insert anything into the fragile port, keeping it in pristine condition for the life of the device.
